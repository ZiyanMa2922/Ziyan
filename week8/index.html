Process & Exploration

Week 8

When working on my Self-Portrait, I first browsed some websites and saw several images that overlapped and had a ghosting effect. I thought back to the Clock we made in previous classes. The images captured by the camera also change continuously over time. If I saved previous frames in an array and overlaid them with the real-time camera footage, I could create a ghosting effect. I thought this would create a very interesting visual effect. I used blue and pink to draw images from two different moments in time. I used the mouse's X coordinate to control how much the two layers overlap, and the mouse's Y coordinate to control the brightness threshold. The larger the Y value, the higher the brightness threshold, and the fewer areas are drawn. Then I started thinking about how to make this work feel more like my own self-portrait. I wanted to change the parts of my hair that I had dyed yellow to white, so I tried using RGB values to identify the yellow areas. However, because my hair color and skin tone are somewhat similar, when the lighting is brighter, some parts of my skin are also identified as yellow and turn white.
