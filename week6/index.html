Process & Exploration

1. Week 6

In this week's assignment, I used an array to create a collection of stars with different sizes and transparency levels. Each time I click the mouse, pop() removes one star from the array. As the number of stars gradually decreases, I wanted to create the feeling that the sky is slowly getting brighter. When the last star disappears, the background changes from black to sky blue, just like the sky at dawn. I also used many small, transparent circles to form the shapes of clouds, allowing them to slowly float across the daytime sky.
